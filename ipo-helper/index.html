STEP BY STEP

How to check an allotment

  1. 1
    Check the official issue timetable

    Use the issuer's or exchange's announcement. Dates can change.

  2. 2
    Confirm the registrar

    Use the registrar named in the offer documents for that issue.

  3. 3
    Open its official status page

    Check the address bar and select the correct issue before entering anything.

  4. 4
    Review the result and bank status

    If the result is not posted, check later through official channels. Your broker or bank can help with application or mandate questions.
